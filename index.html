Foundation and Organisational Development

Soma Consultants was established in 1984, focusing on organisational development, leadership effectiveness, team performance and change management. Early work supported organisations across the UK, helping develop the participative and behavioural approaches that remain central to Soma’s methodology today.

When Clients Engage Soma

When progress depends on people working differently together.

Clients engage Soma when strategic or operational progress depends on stronger leadership, closer collaboration and clearer organisational alignment.

Organisational change

Soma aligns leadership, culture and behaviour around a new direction, combining facilitated workshops with individual coaching so change takes hold in how people actually work, not just on paper.

Major programmes

Soma creates the collaboration and accountability major programmes depend on, working directly with programme leadership to align priorities across partner organisations and keep delivery on track.

Acquisition and integration

Leadership and team performance

Our Expertise

Expertise built around complex organisational challenges.

Soma combines six complementary capabilities to address challenges that cannot be solved through process, structure or training alone.

Leadership and Executive Development

The Challenge

Leaders moving into more senior or more complex roles need more than technical skill to succeed.

What Soma Does

Provides coaching and development shaped around the individual and the organisation around them.

The Outcome

Leaders who think strategically, act practically and lead with confidence.

Organisational Change and Culture

The Challenge

Change that looks right on paper often fails to take hold in how people actually work.

What Soma Does

Works through the transition itself, not just its design, so new behaviours and structures stick.

The Outcome

Change that survives beyond the programme, with the culture to sustain it.

Team Performance and Collaboration

The Challenge

Teams under delivery pressure lose trust and struggle to work through conflict productively.

What Soma Does

Builds the understanding and behaviours needed for teams to work through friction and deliver together.

The Outcome

Stronger collaboration and teams that hold together under real pressure.

Major Programme Performance

The Challenge

Complex programmes fail when leadership is fragmented and partner organisations work to different priorities.

What Soma Does

Creates the alignment, behaviours and accountability required for teams to deliver as one.

The Outcome

Programmes that start well, stay aligned and deliver as planned.

Strategy and Implementation

The Challenge

A strategy that is well argued is not yet matched by the capability or commitment needed to deliver it.

What Soma Does

Works with leadership teams to define direction, then builds the capability and commitment to deliver it.

The Outcome

A strategy the organisation acts on, not one that sits in a document.

Stakeholder Alignment and Facilitation

The Challenge

Complex decisions stall when stakeholders hold different priorities and no shared forum exists to resolve them.

What Soma Does

Facilitates the conversations that bring the right people together and turn insight into agreed action.

The Outcome

Clear priorities, aligned stakeholders and a shared starting point for change.
