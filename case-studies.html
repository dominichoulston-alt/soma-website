Acquisition & Integration

Leadership transformation following an international acquisition

42%
Revenue growth over three years · two year engagement · EBIT maintained above 10%
The Challenge
Following an acquisition, an Australian based global engineering organisation needed to integrate a relationship driven workforce into a larger corporate structure, while preserving the client relationships, expertise and entrepreneurial culture that had driven its success. Priorities included retaining talent, breaking down silos, strengthening governance and delivering ambitious growth targets while maintaining profitability.
The Intervention
Soma partnered with the leadership team to place people and culture at the centre of the integration strategy. Through facilitated leadership workshops, team transformation programmes and individual coaching over a two year period, we helped build accountability, stronger collaboration and a shared vision for success, alongside clearer roles, stakeholder management and customer relationship skills.
The Result
Successful transition of leadership following the departure of the founding managing partner. 66% of leadership vacancies filled internally, supported through coaching and development. A high trust, high accountability leadership culture with over 90% on-time completion of agreed actions. A clear business strategy, governance framework and stronger customer-focused culture supporting business growth.
“Taking over the reins was never going to be easy, but Soma’s coaching helped me find my own leadership style and create strong new relationships with people I’d worked alongside for years.”
